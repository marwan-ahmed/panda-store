# SPEC — TASK-002

> Source of truth for acceptance. Every AC traces to EXEC_PROMPT (`← EXEC §x` and/or `R<n>`).
> The phase tool rejects an AC line without that reference.

## 1. Summary
Add a browser-persisted Arabic RTL shopping cart to the Panda Store (`باندا ستور`) demo storefront. Visitors can add any of the 10 approved catalog products from the home catalog (`/`) or product details (`/products/[id]`) pages, access an independent cart page (`/cart`) via a persistent top header cart button displaying the total item count (`itemCount`), adjust quantities within `1`–`99`, remove items or clear the cart, and view exact integer Iraqi Dinar (`د.ع`) line subtotals and grand total with zero server sessions, databases, checkout forms, or payment gateways.

## 2. Acceptance criteria
- **AC-1** (← EXEC §2.1, §2.2, §4 E1, R1, R9): Given the 10-product synthetic catalog (`PRODUCTS` with IDs `"1"` through `"10"`), when any store page (`/`, `/products/[id]`, `/cart`, or not-found) is rendered inside `RootLayout`, then `SiteHeader` renders at the top with a link (`<a href="/cart">`) showing the live total piece counter (`itemCount`, initially `0`); and every product card on `/` (`ProductCard`) and product details view on `/products/[id]` (`ProductDetails`) renders an Arabic `"أضف إلى السلة"` button.
- **AC-2** (← EXEC §2.1, §4 E1, E2, R1, R2): Given an empty cart (`itemCount = 0`, `cartTotalIqd = 0`), when a visitor adds product `"1"` (`"كوب باندا خزفي"`, `15,000 د.ع`) via `addToCart` or by clicking `"أضف إلى السلة"` on `/` or `/products/1`, then the cart contains 1 line (`productId: "1"`, `quantity: 1`, `lineSubtotalIqd: 15000` formatted `"15,000 د.ع"`), `itemCount` becomes `1`, and `cartTotalIqd` becomes `15000` (`"15,000 د.ع"`); when product `"1"` is added a second time, it stays on a single line with `quantity: 2`, `lineSubtotalIqd: 30000` (`"30,000 د.ع"`), `itemCount: 2`, and `cartTotalIqd: 30000` (`"30,000 د.ع"`).
- **AC-3** (← EXEC §2.3, §4 E6, E7, E8, R2, R4): Given a cart containing product `"1"` (`15,000 د.ع`), when its quantity is updated via `updateCartQuantity` or the `/cart` quantity input to `99`, then `quantity` becomes `99` and `lineSubtotalIqd` becomes `1485000` (`"1,485,000 د.ع"`); when adding or incrementing at `quantity: 99` (to `100`), the update is rejected, `quantity` remains `99` (`"1,485,000 د.ع"`), and a clear Arabic alert states the maximum limit (`99`) is reached; and when a cart item has `quantity: 2` and invalid inputs (`0`, `-3`, `1.5`, `"abc"`, or `""`) are submitted, all invalid inputs are rejected without rounding or arithmetic error, leaving `quantity: 2` (`"30,000 د.ع"`) intact with an Arabic validation message.
- **AC-4** (← EXEC §2.3, §2.4, §4 E4, E5, E9, R5): Given an item in the cart at `quantity: 1`, when the decrement action (`decrementCartItem` or the decrement button) is invoked, then `quantity` remains `1` and the item is not removed (decrement button disabled at `1`); given a cart with product `"2"` (`9,500 د.ع × 2 = 19,000 د.ع`) and product `"9"` (`7,500 د.ع × 3 = 22,500 د.ع`), when `"حذف"` (`removeFromCart`) is triggered for product `"2"`, then product `"2"` is completely removed, product `"9"` remains, `itemCount` becomes `3`, and `cartTotalIqd` becomes `22500` (`"22,500 د.ع"`); and when `"إفراغ السلة"` (`clearCart`) is triggered, all items are removed leaving `itemCount: 0` and `cartTotalIqd: 0` (`"0 د.ع"`).
- **AC-5** (← EXEC §2.5, §4 E3, E6, E13, R6): Given a cart containing product `"2"` (`9,500 د.ع × 2`) and product `"9"` (`7,500 د.ع × 3`), when totals are computed via `buildCart` and formatted via `formatCartTotalIqd`, then `lineSubtotalIqd` values are `19000` (`"19,000 د.ع"`) and `22500` (`"22,500 د.ع"`), `itemCount` is `5`, and `cartTotalIqd` is `41500` (`"41,500 د.ع"`) with zero delivery fees, taxes, coupons, or discounts; and given all 10 catalog products (`"1"`–`"10"`) each at `quantity: 99`, `itemCount` equals `990` and `cartTotalIqd` equals `19899000` (`"19,899,000 د.ع"`) as an exact safe integer without overflow, while `src/lib/products.ts#formatPriceIqd` continues rejecting `0` and `formatCartTotalIqd(0)` returns `"0 د.ع"`.
- **AC-6** (← EXEC §2.2, §4 E4, R7, R9): Given the independent cart route `/cart` (`src/app/cart/page.tsx`), when visited with an empty cart (initially, after removing the last item, or after clearing), then `/cart` renders `<h1>سلة التسوق</h1>`, `itemCount` `0`, total `"0 د.ع"`, a clear Arabic empty-cart message, and a `<a href="/">` link back to the catalog; and when populated, `/cart` renders each line item's Arabic `name`, local `/images/products/...svg` image with Arabic `imageAlt`, catalog `priceIqd` (`"<n> د.ع"`), `quantity`, `lineSubtotalIqd` (`"<n> د.ع"`), quantity adjustment and `"حذف"` controls, `"إفراغ السلة"`, `cartTotalIqd`, and a link back to `/`.
- **AC-7** (← EXEC §2.2, §4 E11, R8): Given product `"1"` added with `quantity: 2`, when inspecting `localStorage` under key `panda-store:cart:v1` (`CART_STORAGE_KEY`), then only allow-listed `{ version: 1, items: [{ productId: "1", quantity: 2 }] }` JSON is stored (with zero prices, descriptions, names, secrets, or PII); and when navigating between `/`, `/products/1`, and `/cart`, reloading/remounting from `localStorage`, or receiving a `storage` event, the cart restores product `"1"` with `quantity: 2`, `itemCount: 2`, and `"30,000 د.ع"` without a database, server session, or `cookies()`.
- **AC-8** (← EXEC §3 R3, §4 E10, E12, §7, R3): Given untrusted inputs or corrupted `localStorage` data, when `addToCart`, `updateCartQuantity`, `sanitizeCartItems`, or `parseStoredCart` runs with an unknown/empty ID (`"99"`, `""`, `"__proto__"`) or tampered fields (`{ productId: "1", quantity: 2, priceIqd: 1 }`), then unknown IDs are rejected/dropped and unit prices and display metadata resolve strictly from `PRODUCTS` (`15,000 د.ع` for product `"1"`); completely malformed `localStorage` strings recover to an empty cart (`itemCount: 0`, `"0 د.ع"`) with zero runtime errors; mixed arrays containing `{ productId: "1", quantity: 2, priceIqd: 1 }`, `{ productId: "99", quantity: 1 }`, and invalid quantities (`0`, `150`, `1.5`) drop all invalid/unknown entries and keep product `"1"` (`quantity: 2`, `"30,000 د.ع"`); and duplicate valid entries for the same `productId` merge by summing quantities up to max `99`.
- **AC-9** (← EXEC §2.6, §5, §7, R10): Given all storefront routes, components, and server/proxy modules, when inspected after implementation, then there are zero checkout or order-submission `<form>`s, zero customer data inputs (name, phone, address), zero payment gateways or payment keys, zero login/signup/password controls, zero discounts/coupons/taxes/delivery fees, zero search/filter/sort/stock UI, zero `"use server"` Server Actions, zero `src/app/api/**/route.ts` route handlers, and zero `cookies()`/`Set-Cookie` usage; and existing catalog routes (`/`, `/products/1`–`/products/10`, and HTTP `404` on unknown `/products/*`) remain fully intact, while TASK-001 tests that barred cart UI (`tests/home.test.tsx#AC-8`, `tests/security-assets.test.ts#AB-2`) are updated in `TESTS` to allow the new cart controls while preserving checkout/payment/auth/search prohibitions.
- **AC-10** (← EXEC §3 R11, §7, R11): Given the root layout, `/cart` metadata, cart components, and tests, when rendered or inspected, then `<html lang="ar" dir="rtl">` is preserved, all cart headings, labels, buttons, alerts, and empty-state copy are in Arabic, no external `http://`/`https://` network requests are made, `package.json` runtime `dependencies` remain strictly `["next", "react", "react-dom"]`, and all code and tests contain exclusively synthetic data with zero real PII.

## 3. Edge cases
- **EC-1** (→ AC-2, AC-8): Passing empty (`""`), whitespace (`"   "`), unknown (`"99"`, `"0"`, `"-1"`), or `Object.prototype` keys (`"__proto__"`, `"constructor"`, `"toString"`, `"valueOf"`) as `productId` to `addToCart`, `updateCartQuantity`, `removeFromCart`, or `sanitizeCartItems` fails closed via `getProductById` without mutating state or leaking prototype properties.
- **EC-2** (→ AC-3): Quantity parsing and validation (`validateCartQuantity`, `parseQuantityInput`) accept exact integer boundaries `1` and `99`, and reject `0`, `-1`, `-3`, `1.5`, `0.5`, `"1abc"`, `"1e2"`, `""`, `"   "`, `100`, `NaN`, `Infinity`, `-Infinity`, `Number.MAX_SAFE_INTEGER + 1`, and non-number/non-string values without rounding, truncating, or mutating existing valid quantity (`2` or `99`).
- **EC-3** (→ AC-4): Decrementing an item at `quantity: 1` (`decrementCartItem`) preserves `quantity: 1` (`reason: "min_quantity_reached"`) and never deletes the item; removing the sole remaining item or calling `clearCart` transitions cleanly to `itemCount: 0` and `"0 د.ع"`.
- **EC-4** (→ AC-5): `formatCartTotalIqd` accepts `0` (`"0 د.ع"`), `750` (`"750 د.ع"`), `41500` (`"41,500 د.ع"`), `1485000` (`"1,485,000 د.ع"`), and `19899000` (`"19,899,000 د.ع"`), and throws `TypeError`/`RangeError` on `-1`, `15000.5`, `NaN`, `Infinity`, or unsafe integers; meanwhile `src/lib/products.ts#formatPriceIqd(0)` still throws.
- **EC-5** (→ AC-8): Corrupted or hostile `localStorage` payloads — malformed JSON (`"{bad"`), oversized strings (`> 65536` chars), wrong schema (`null`, `123`, `{ version: 99, items: [] }`), JSON containing `__proto__` keys or `<script>` strings in extra fields (`priceIqd: 1`, `name: "<script>alert(1)</script>"`), valid duplicate entries summing past `99` (`[{ productId: "1", quantity: 60 }, { productId: "1", quantity: 50 }]` → `quantity: 99`), or `localStorage` throwing `SecurityError`/`QuotaExceededError` — never crash, never execute scripts, never reflect tampered strings, and fall back gracefully.
- **EC-6** (→ AC-1, AC-6, AC-7): Under Next.js 16 `cacheComponents: true`, initial SSR and hydration snapshots (`getServerCartSnapshot`) deterministically emit empty cart (`0`, `"0 د.ع"`) without React hydration mismatch, then synchronize immediately to `localStorage` state across `<Activity>` route hiding/showing and standalone Vitest component renders (even without `<CartProvider>`).

## 4. Out of scope
- Server-side cart storage, database persistence, cart REST/GraphQL endpoints, or server sessions (`EXEC §5`, `Q1: أ`).
- Slide-over cart drawer/modal instead of the independent `/cart` page (`EXEC §5`, `Q2: أ`).
- Checkout flow, order submission, or customer PII forms (name, phone number, delivery address).
- Cash-on-delivery execution, online payment gateways, or payment API keys.
- User authentication, accounts, roles, or user-linked carts.
- Discounts, coupons, compare-at/strikethrough prices, taxes, or delivery/shipping fees.
- Modifying the 10 synthetic catalog products, adding inventory/stock tracking, or adding search/filter/sort/reviews.

## 5. Non-functional requirements
- **Security & data hygiene:** Zero trust in `localStorage` or DOM inputs; allow-listed `{ productId, quantity }` persistence only; exclusive price/metadata resolution from static `PRODUCTS`; zero payment keys, `.env*` secrets, real PII, external `http(s)://` URLs, `"use server"` actions, `api/**` routes, or `cookies()` (`EXEC §7`, `AGENTS.md`).
- **Localization & layout:** Arabic UI (`lang="ar"`, `dir="rtl"` in `RootLayout`) across all headers, buttons, cart lines, alerts, empty states, and route metadata (`EXEC §7`, `R11`).
- **Monetary & runtime discipline:** All IQD unit prices, subtotals, and cart totals computed strictly as safe integers (`Number.isSafeInteger`); zero new npm runtime dependencies; full compatibility with Next.js 16 `cacheComponents: true` and `partialPrefetching: true` (`EXEC §7`, `AGENTS.md`).
